$ Energy for Growth Hub · Making Markets Work
What is this? The DFC Tracker tallies all active commitments since the launch of the US International Development Finance Corporation in January 2020. All projects can be parsed by sector, country income tier, or region.

Why do we need this? With an investment cap of $205 billion, America’s development finance agency has a dual mandate to advance US foreign policy and economic development around the world. The tracker allows analysis of how and where DFC resources are committed.

HUB ANALYSIS: THE ENERGY SHARE OF DFC’S PORTFOLIO

Based on Energy for Growth Hub analysis. How we define “energy” ▸

How we define “energy”: With each new quarterly DFC data update, we analyze every DFC project’s name and description using 16 key energy terms. We then manually confirm each project’s energy focus. The Energy Share encompasses all types of energy projects including electricity generation, transmission lines, financing for off-grid power, and oil production, among others.
